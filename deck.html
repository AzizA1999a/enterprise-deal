The Monday-morning list
  • 1Map who you already know inside, and who can introduce you.A warm path beats a better pitch.
  • 2Find the buyer's hidden priority and their boss's rule before you quote.The sellers who knew them in our game wrote better term sheets.
  • 3Bring proof the buyer can show upward.One reference customer, one certification, one number.
  • 4Offer a small first step.A pilot the buyer can defend if it fails.
  • 5Do not lead with a discount.You are competing with "do nothing", not with a price.
